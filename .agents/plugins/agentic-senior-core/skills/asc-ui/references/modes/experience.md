# Surface Mode: Experience (Showcase, Media & Portfolios)

Use for: Creative portfolios, design galleries, interactive product demos, and media showcases.

> **Core Principle:** The visitor is inside the work itself. Interface chrome recedes entirely so the visual artifact, artwork, or interaction leads from the first viewport. The UI serves as a quiet frame for the subject.

---

## 1. Canvas Treatment & Spatial Composition

- **Receding Interface Chrome:**
  - Navigation headers, footers, and control sidebars should be lightweight, semi-transparent or edge-aligned, maximizing the uninhibited canvas area.
  - Background surface: Neutral darkroom or gallery tint (`bg-neutral-950` or warm museum white `bg-stone-50`) chosen specifically to elevate the subject's colors without competition.
- **Full-Bleed Media Presentation:**
  - Let high-resolution media fill containers naturally with `object-cover` or `object-contain`.
  - Provide full-screen zoom or modal lightbox views for high-resolution visual inspection.

---

## 2. Interaction & Micro-Choreography

- **Tactile Exploration:**
  - Interactive previews, drag-to-compare sliders, or spatial hover panning.
  - Smooth, seamless transitions between overview grid cards and full-fidelity artifact inspection.
- **Restraint Over Distraction:**
  - Never add cartoonish particle effects, cursor trails, or autoplaying audio. The craftsmanship of the showcased work is the emotional center.
